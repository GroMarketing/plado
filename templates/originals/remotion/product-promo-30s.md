Use the Remotion skill. Create a 30-second product promo for ${Product:our app} at ${Size:1080x1920}, 30fps.

Beats, in order:
1. Hook (0 to 3s): the problem in the viewer's words, with a word they identify with in the first frame.
2. Problem (3 to 8s): one concrete example of the pain.
3. Product (8 to 18s): show ${Product:our app} doing the job in 2 or 3 short scenes. Use real UI screenshots from ${ScreenshotsFolder:./public/screens} and a gentle zoom, not a spinning device.
4. Proof (18 to 25s): one number, "${ProofStat:3x faster onboarding}". Animate it with a count-up. If you compare numbers with bars, draw bars only when every number shares the same unit.
5. Call to action (25 to 30s): "${CTA:Start free today}" and the URL, held for at least 3 seconds.

Style: ${Style:clean, dark background, one accent color}. Use the brand fonts in ${FontsFolder:./public/fonts}. Keep text inside the platform safe zone and hold each text beat for (characters / 15) seconds plus 1 second.
